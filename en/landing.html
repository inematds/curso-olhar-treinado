How to study

Read a lesson and set aside ten minutes for the activity. Your progress stays in this browser. Export your journey to make a copy.

The images are illustrations generated in Codex. Practices use your own photos, paper, or drawings. Image generation is optional; you don’t need to pay for a tool.

Who it is for

It is for you if you

  • You create images, events, or communications and want to explain your visual choices.
  • You use photos on your phone and have little time per session.
  • Prefer to learn by doing.

It is not for you if you

  • Already know the subject well and want technical material.
  • Want a traditional video lesson.
